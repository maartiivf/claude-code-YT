# English faceless long-form "outlier" channels in high-RPM money/tech niches (2025-2026)

Method note (applies to all channel stats below): every subscriber count, video count, view count, duration and publish date was pulled directly from YouTube on 2026-09-29. Channel pages were read at `https://www.youtube.com/@HANDLE/videos`, sorted with the "Popular" and "Oldest" chips. Exact publish dates and view counts came from the `youtubei/v1/next` endpoint. Original titles were confirmed via YouTube oEmbed. Candidates were found with ~90 YouTube searches filtered to "this year + video + 4-20 min / 20+ min", sorted by views, plus relevance-sorted searches on proven title patterns. About 900 channels were screened by subscriber count.

For faceless status and visual style, I looked at each channel's top-video thumbnail plus YouTube's auto-generated frames at ~25/50/75% of the video (`i.ytimg.com/vi/ID/hq1-3.jpg`). That is a 3-frame sample, not a full watch. "Ratio" = views of the channel's top video / current subscribers. Views are rounded; subscribers are as displayed by YouTube.

## Q1. Which specific English faceless channels match the outlier profile (few subs, recent viral long-form videos)?

### Takeaway
There are plenty of small English faceless channels (3K-270K subs) whose 2025-2026 long-form videos reach 0.3M-4M views, with ratios of 15x to 600x. The densest clusters are:
- business "rise & fall / mistake" case studies,
- "Explained Like You're 5" cartoon finance,
- "richest family / old money" wealth documentaries,
- car-brand-collapse videos,
- AI-economy explainers.

Many channels started in late 2025 or 2026, and in several cases the first or second upload was the viral hit.

### Gaps
- None of the RPM figures are audited. All come from tool vendors or blogs estimating from "published ranges", not from creator revenue disclosures.
